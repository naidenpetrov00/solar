# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective residential customers and business decision-makers evaluating solar installations. On the welcome page, both audiences need an immediate, equally prominent path to relevant information.

## Product Purpose

The site explains solar solutions, builds confidence in the company, and helps qualified visitors move toward an enquiry. The welcome page succeeds when a visitor immediately understands that the company builds solar systems and can choose between home and business content.

## Positioning

No verified company-specific differentiator is currently available. Use accurate solar-design-and-installation language without inventing claims.

## Operating Context

This is a public marketing website serving organic, referral, and paid-ad traffic, with a strong mobile audience. It may serve visitors in Bulgaria and the European Economic Area.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The welcome page provides equal Home and Business paths to same-page sections.
- Bulgarian is the source language; English, Turkish, and Ukrainian are supported.
- The site uses Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Vercel.
- Do not invent pricing, savings, guarantees, certifications, testimonials, performance results, or company statistics.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the current `Solar` brand label until approved company branding is supplied.
- The approved welcome-page direction is “Horizon Gate”: a premium dark-and-white, full-viewport photographic hero with two equal destination rails.
- The interface should feel modern, technically credible, distinctive, and restrained.

## Evidence on Hand

No verified project photography, testimonials, business metrics, certifications, or detailed brand assets are present in the repository. The welcome hero therefore uses a clearly replaceable temporary licensed photograph.

## Product Principles

- Explain the company’s solar activity immediately and factually.
- Let residential and business visitors self-select without friction.
- Keep the experience fast and focused on mobile.
- Prefer clear, accessible interaction over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals, including keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, comfortable touch targets, meaningful semantics, and reduced-motion support.
