# Marketing, analytics, and consent guidance

Read this file only for marketing, paid advertising, landing pages, conversion, analytics, attribution, tracking, or consent work. Read `PRODUCT.md` too when the task changes positioning or visitor-facing claims.

## Marketing

* Support organic, paid, direct, and referral traffic with a clear purpose, understandable value proposition, sensible CTA hierarchy, message consistency, relevant trust signals, and minimal friction.
* Campaign-specific landing pages are appropriate only when they improve relevance and conversion for a defined audience.
* Do not use misleading claims, fake urgency, fabricated testimonials, manipulative patterns, or unsupported performance claims.

## Analytics and consent

* Keep analytics and advertising integrations isolated from business UI. Track meaningful business actions rather than arbitrary interface interactions.
* Keep the architecture compatible with Google Tag Manager, GA4, Google Ads conversion tracking, Meta Pixel, and future server-side or enhanced tracking when explicitly required.
* Do not add analytics IDs, advertising credentials, or vendor integrations unless requested.
* Assume visitors may be in Bulgaria or the European Economic Area. Keep marketing and analytics consent-aware, do not treat implementation as legal advice, and do not invent legal or privacy wording.
